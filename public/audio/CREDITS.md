The three sword recordings were downmixed to mono, trimmed, peak normalized,
faded, and converted to 16-bit PCM. The completion bell is used as downloaded.
The knife recording was trimmed to the strike at 5.36–6.00 seconds, downmixed to
mono, peak normalized, faded, and converted to 16-bit PCM.
Runtime: sword samples decode once per AudioContext and are served from the site's
own hosting. The completion bell plays on boss victories in Parry and the final
winner screen in Pirate Barrel; the knife-on-wood sound plays as the colored knife
finishes entering the barrel.
